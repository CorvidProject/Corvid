Description of Service

Corvid is an Ethereum-native trading platform that provides:

  • On-chain token analysis and risk scoring

Auto Trader executes inside the caps you set (daily ETH spend, per-trade size, max trades per day). Swaps proposed by the AI agent require your approval in Telegram.
