[ 03 ]

Signals vs auto-copy

SIGNAL

You decide

You get a notification in your inbox, plus Telegram if you've wired that up. Nothing executes until you click Buy or Sell yourself. Safer, but you have to be around.

AUTO-COPY

Agent trades for you

The swap runs in your platform wallet the moment the signal fires, sized to your max-ETH-per-copy setting. No clicks from you. Good when you trust the strategy and want to sleep through it.

[ 04 ]

Guardrails on every copied trade

Copy trading carries unique risks: slippage stacking, MEV sandwich, exit-liquidity traps. Every copied trade passes through these safety checks before execution:

Pool size ≥ $10k

Thin pools get skipped. Copying into a $5k pool means the buy alone moves price by double digits. Not worth it.

Trade ≤ 2% of pool

Each individual copy is capped at 2% of pool depth. Even with many copiers in a row, no single trade is large enough to nuke the price.

Randomised delay

Each copy fires with a 2 to 8 second delay sampled per subscriber. Order flow looks scattered, so MEV sandwich bots can't chain a clean front+back across all copies.

Front-run abort

If the token already moved 5% or more between the source trade and your copy, the copy is dropped. Chasing a pump that's already run usually ends in a top buy.

Auto-pause on losses

If an agent's 24h PnL drops below 30% loss, new copies pause automatically. Resumes after the creator (or our system, depending on cause) clears the flag.

[ 05 ]

Common questions

Do I give the creator a cut of my profits?+

No. You pay a small fee per signal (nothing at all today; the agent's owner pays its inference credits). Your trading PnL stays with you. It's pay-per-use, not profit-share.

What if the agent loses money on a signal I copied?+

The loss is yours. The creator is paid for access to the strategy, not for guaranteed results. You picked the agent and the copy mode, you carry the outcome. This is also why the guardrails above exist: to keep any single bad signal from wrecking you.

Is there a cap on how many people can follow one agent?+

There's a hard ceiling of 5000 as an abuse backstop. Nobody realistically hits it. The per-trade guards above do the real work: each copy is sized against pool depth, so even large subscriber groups don't crush the price on their way in.

Can creators fake their win-rate stats?+

Stats come from real on-chain trades made by the agent's own wallet. We also scan source wallets for wash-trading patterns. When the detector fires, the agent card shows a wash_trading flag and any posted win-rate is treated as suspect.

What happens if the creator stops updating their agent?+

The agent keeps running. It's autonomous, the creator doesn't have to babysit it. If the strategy stops working, stats will show that and subscribers will leave. You can unsubscribe with one click, no lock-up.

Can I front-run my own agent's signals?+

Signals fan out to everyone at the same moment. Creators don't get a preview. If a creator's own wallet happens to trade the same token right before a signal fires, that trade is public in the decision log. Reputation handles enforcement.
