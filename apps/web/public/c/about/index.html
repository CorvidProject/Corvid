Company

Corvid is a trading technology platform. Operated by the Corvid team, which operates the service at corvid.example.

We are a small engineering team. The product is built and run by the people who write it, and the address below reaches them rather than a queue.

[ 02 ]

What We Build

Autonomous on-chain trading agents and analysis tools for Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain and Solana. The platform lets you assess a token before touching it, follow wallets that trade well, and run automated strategies from a wallet you control.

Agents execute from their own wallets, funded by you and withdrawable by you at any time. We do not take custody of funds beyond what you deliberately move into an agent.
