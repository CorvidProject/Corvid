[ 01 ]

Introduction

Corvid operates the platform at corvid.example. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is handled.

Using Corvid constitutes acceptance. If you disagree, discontinue use.

[ 02 ]

What We Collect

Account

  • Wallet address, or email address and password hash if you sign up with email
  • Signed authentication message (ephemeral, discarded after verification)
  • Display name, avatar, bio (optional, set by you)
  • Trading preferences and notification settings

Platform wallet

  • Public key, stored for display and on-chain calls
  • Private key, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a server-side key. It is used to sign only on your instruction or by agents you configured
  • Connected external wallets (MetaMask, Rabby): public key only, never the private key

Usage

  • Agent configurations, pipelines, and run history
  • Token analysis requests and verdicts returned by the AI
  • Smart-money watchlists and alerts
  • Signal subscriptions and outcomes
  • Credit and $CRVD ledger entries (balance, top-ups, fees, distributions)
  • Marketplace activity: published agents, clones, subscribers, revenue share
  • Server logs: IP, user agent, pages visited, request ids

Optional integrations

  • Telegram chat id for signal delivery (you provide it)
  • X (Twitter) OAuth tokens when you enable auto-posting on an agent. Stored encrypted. Used only for the posts you authorise
[ 03 ]

How We Use Your Information

  • Platform functionality: run agents, deliver signals, execute trades, manage your account
  • AI analysis: on-chain metrics and aggregate stats are sent to OpenAI. Not personally identifiable
  • Billing: track credit and $CRVD balances, distribute marketplace revenue to agent creators, route fees to the platform fee pool
  • Security: detect fraud, abuse, bot traffic, and unauthorised access
  • Communication: transactional messages only (alerts, signal delivery, critical account events). No marketing without consent
  • Improvement: aggregated, anonymised usage patterns to tune features and model quality
[ 04 ]

AI Analysis Disclaimer

※ Notice
AI-generated analysis is not financial advice. Outputs are informational only.

You are responsible for your trading decisions. Crypto trading carries substantial risk. Past agent or signal performance does not predict future results.

[ 05 ]

Non-Custodial Wallet Statement

The platform wallet is custodial: the encrypted private key is stored on our infrastructure so agents can sign while you are offline. You can export the key at any time. Treat your Corvid credentials like a hardware wallet seed.

We strongly recommend:

  • Export and back up your private key from Settings
  • Use a strong, unique sign-in wallet
  • Enable 2FA on the linked wallet when available
  • Do not hold large balances on the platform wallet
[ 06 ]

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with:

  • Supabase: database and session auth
  • Alchemy: Ethereum RPC and webhook infrastructure
  • GeckoTerminal, DexScreener, GoPlus: token price, holder, trade history, and contract risk lookups
  • 0x: DEX routing and swap quotes
  • Flashbots: transaction bundle submission for priority execution
  • OpenAI: receives token on-chain metrics for analysis. No personal identifiers
  • Telegram / X: only when you explicitly connect the integration, and only for the specific actions you authorise
  • Hosting: VPS provider running the application and edge cache in front of it
  • Law enforcement: when compelled by valid legal process
[ 07 ]

Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. On deletion:

  • Profile and preference data: removed within 30 days
  • Agent configs, pipelines, and subscriptions: deleted immediately
  • Ledger and outcome history: retained up to 90 days for security, accounting, and dispute resolution, then anonymised or deleted
  • Encrypted private keys: deleted with the account. Withdraw funds first
[ 08 ]

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate records
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain processing

All of these requests are handled through the Settings page inside the application.

[ 09 ]

Cookies

Session cookies only, for authentication. No tracking, no ad cookies. Disabling cookies blocks sign-in.

[ 10 ]

Children

Corvid is not for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe we have, raise it from inside the app and the account will be removed.

[ 11 ]

Changes to This Policy

Policy updates are announced on-platform and in transactional email where available. Continued use after the change constitutes acceptance.
